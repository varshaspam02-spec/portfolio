      How it works

      Forward. The drawing is shrunk to 28 × 28 = 784 input values, passed through one ReLU hidden layer and turned into 10 class probabilities by a softmax.

      Backward. Cross-entropy loss is differentiated by hand: backpropagation yields ∂L/∂W2 and ∂L/∂W1, and mini-batch SGD moves every weight against its gradient.

      Start. Weights begin as He or Xavier noise, then a short warm start on font-rendered digits gives the network a rough idea of each class.
