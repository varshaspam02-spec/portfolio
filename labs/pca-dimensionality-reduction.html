Variance kept is the share of the cloud's total variance that survives the drop from three dimensions to two.

How it works

PCA looks for the directions along which the data varies most. It centres the points on their mean, builds the 3 × 3 covariance matrix, then runs power iteration: multiply a random vector by the matrix and normalise, again and again, until it settles on the dominant eigenvector, PC1. Deflation removes that direction so the next pass finds PC2, then PC3. Keeping only the PC1 and PC2 coordinates turns every 3D point into a 2D one while giving up as little variance as possible.
