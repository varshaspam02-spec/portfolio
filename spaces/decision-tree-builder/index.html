Use the sample data, paste a CSV, or upload your own. Select a target, adjust the tree settings, and build. Inspect a row or export the model.

What to look for
  • Greedy CART-style splitting for numeric and categorical features.
  • Gini impurity and entropy for classification; MSE for regression.
  • Depth, sample-count, and gain limits that control tree complexity.
