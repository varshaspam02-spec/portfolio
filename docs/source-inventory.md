## Design findings

The original site mixes light page backgrounds with independently styled iframe applications. Oversized headers and large empty areas separate the identity from the useful work. Projects are presented as a long undifferentiated list, with inconsistent heading levels. The redesign introduces a consistent navigation system, clearer page hierarchy, project filters and search, a visual lab directory, and on-demand local lab loading.

## Accuracy and scope

- The contact-page bio is the source for the five-plus years of experience, Nuremberg location, RICHES, and current blackbox testing work. No employers, degrees, dates, performance benchmarks, or availability claims were invented.
- The manifold embed’s `applyManifoldLearning` function uses geometric heuristics. The new page and standalone application identify it as a concept demonstration.
- The attention embed’s generated patterns and next-token probabilities are simulated. Both entry points label them as illustrative.
- The technical guides were condensed into practical controls and key concepts. Unverified production comparisons in the old explanatory copy were omitted.
- The external object-detection app and both article destinations remain external. The seven internal labs and clustering comparison table are hosted with the new portfolio.
- No contact form backend, analytics, cookies, or authentication was added. The only local storage is the animation preference.
